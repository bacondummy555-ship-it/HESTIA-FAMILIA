Rules and Regulations

Every member of Hestia Familia is expected to follow these rules to preserve respect, unity, and order within our community.

✦

Code of the Familia

✦

Respect the banner. Respect the Familia.

I

Respect Everyone

Treat every member with respect. Harassment, bullying, discrimination, personal attacks, and unnecessary hostility are not allowed.

II

No Spamming

Avoid repeated messages, excessive mentions, unnecessary emojis, disruptive soundboards, and flooding channels with unrelated content.

III

Use the Correct Channels

Keep conversations, media, commands, promotions, and game-related content inside their proper channels.

IV

No Inappropriate Content

Do not post explicit, disturbing, illegal, harmful, or unsafe content anywhere inside the server.

V

No Advertising Without Permission

Do not promote other servers, communities, social accounts, products, or services unless permission has been given by the staff.

VI

Protect Member Privacy

Never share another member's personal details, private messages, photos, or information without their permission.

VII

Follow Staff Instructions

Respect decisions made by the Owner, Admins, and Staff. Concerns may be raised calmly through the correct support or appeal channel.

VIII

No Exploiting or Cheating

Do not encourage exploits, scams, account theft, cheating tools, malicious files, or activities that could harm other players.

IX

Represent Hestia Properly

Members carrying the Hestia name, tag, or role should behave responsibly both inside and outside the server.

X

Use Common Sense

Not every harmful action can be listed. Avoid behavior that damages the community, creates conflict, or takes advantage of other members.

⚔

Disciplinary Action

Violations may result in a warning, timeout, role removal, temporary suspension, or permanent removal depending on the severity and frequency of the offense.
